Our Chapel

A place of worship, reflection, and spiritual growth.

Faith & Community

Our chapel stands as the spiritual heart of St. Ann's School, offering students and staff a sacred space for prayer, reflection, and worship. We believe in nurturing not only academic excellence but also spiritual growth.

Regular services and special celebrations bring our community together in faith, regardless of denomination or background.

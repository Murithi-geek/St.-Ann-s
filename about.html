About St. Ann's School

Learn about our rich history, mission, and commitment to excellence in education.

Our Mission

St. Ann's School is committed to providing a nurturing environment where students can develop academically, socially, and spiritually. We strive to prepare our students to become responsible global citizens who contribute positively to society through excellence in character and service.

Excellence in Learning

Academics

Our academic programs are designed to challenge students to reach their full potential. With small class sizes, dedicated teachers, and a comprehensive curriculum, we provide students with the knowledge and skills they need to succeed in higher education and beyond. Our graduates consistently achieve outstanding results in national examinations and gain acceptance to prestigious universities.

Small class sizes Qualified teachers Modern facilities
Resource-Rich Environment

Library

Our state-of-the-art library serves as the intellectual hub of our school. With an extensive collection of books, digital resources, and dedicated study areas, it provides students with all the tools they need for research and independent learning. Our librarians work closely with teachers to ensure students develop strong information literacy skills.

Digital resources Study areas Research support
Developing the Whole Child

Talent Development

We believe that education extends beyond the classroom. Our extensive extracurricular program allows students to explore their interests and develop their talents in sports, arts, music, drama, and many other areas. Through these activities, students learn important life skills such as teamwork, leadership, and perseverance.

Our Core Values

Respect

We value each person's dignity and treat everyone with courtesy and consideration.

Excellence

We strive for the highest standards in all that we do, academically and personally.

Integrity

We act with honesty, accountability, and strong moral principles at all times.

Compassion
